**Full description** (4000 max):

> ZepDriver is a free, offline co-pilot for Mutant Vehicle (art car) drivers in
> Black Rock City.
>
> DRIVE SAFE AND LEGAL
> • A big GPS speedometer with warnings that get louder as you speed up
> • Spoken and on-screen warnings before and inside restricted areas: the
>   Esplanade and promenades (cross, don't drive along), the Great Circle,
>   Center Camp, walk-in camping and the trash fence
> • Rules follow your license: Mutant Vehicle, playa-only, Accessibility
>   Vehicle or bike, coded from each year's DMV rules
>
> FIND YOUR WAY
> • A 3D map of the city with the Man, the Temple and Center Camp
> • Search camps, art and addresses like "4:30 & G"; tap anything for details
> • Multi-stop routes that keep a safe distance from art and follow the rules
>   for your license, with an ETA at playa speed
> • One tap to the nearest potties
> • Your position as a BRC address, favorites and your home camp on the map
>
> STAY TOGETHER
> • Add a $20 LoRa radio (Heltec V3): ZepDriver flashes and sets it up over
>   USB, then every ZepDriver car shares its position and can text the others,
>   with no cell service needed
>
> PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
> • Everything works with no internet
> • No accounts, ads or tracking; your trip log stays on your phone unless you
>   choose a Google backup
> • Camp and art locations appear when Burning Man releases them
>
> This app is not affiliated, endorsed, or verified by Burning Man Project.
> Map data: Burning Man Project GIS. Camp and art data: Burning Man Public API.
