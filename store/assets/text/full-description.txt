Have an art car? Tired of explaining that you can't drive on esplanade or that the big circle around the man is a big NO-NO? People going over 5MPH because speedometers aren't built for that speed? Want to find the nearest porto easily?  Look no further. This app is here to help! All camps, art, porto and driving rules for Burning Man Mutant Vehicles in one spot!

DRIVE SAFE AND LEGAL
• A big GPS speedometer with warnings that get louder as you speed up
• Spoken and on-screen warnings before and inside restricted areas: the Esplanade and promenades (cross, don't drive along), the Great Circle, Center Camp, walk-in camping and the trash fence
• Rules follow your license: Mutant Vehicle, playa-only, Accessibility Vehicle or bike, coded from each year's DMV rules

FIND YOUR WAY
• A 3D map of the city with the Man, the Temple and Center Camp
• Search camps, art and addresses like "4:30 & G"; tap anything for details
• Multi-stop routes that keep a safe distance from art and follow the rules for your license, with an ETA at playa speed
• One tap to the nearest potties
• Your position as a BRC address, favorites and your home camp on the map

STAY TOGETHER
• Add a $20 LoRa radio (Heltec V3): ZepDriver flashes and sets it up over USB, then every ZepDriver car shares its position and can text the others, with no cell service needed

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
• Everything works with no internet
• No accounts, ads or tracking; your trip log stays on your phone unless you choose a Google backup
• Camp and art locations appear when Burning Man releases them
